### 🎉 Initial Release

- 🐢 CPU limits for individual processes or one shared budget for an app and its helpers (100% = one CPU core)
- 🗂️ Process list grouped into apps, background processes and system processes, with helpers shown under their owning app
- 🔍 Search by name or PID and sort by name, PID, CPU usage or limit
- 🌡️ Live CPU readings and a subtle CPU heat map in a native process table
- 🎛️ Context-aware controls for individual processes and app groups, with a link to the owning app
- ⏱️ Adjustable refresh rate (1 s, 2 s, 5 s or paused) and optional pause while pointing at the list
- ⏸️ Pause and resume all CPU limits at once
- 🔁 Optional restoration of app limits after apps restart
- 👋 Welcome on first launch with quick choices for launch at login and automatic update checks
- 🚀 Optional launch at login
- 🔄 Automatic updates via Sparkle, with optional automatic downloads and beta versions
- 🔔 Gentle menu bar reminders when updates are found during background checks
- 🛟 Watchdog protection resumes limited processes if CoreTamer or a controller quits unexpectedly
- 🌐 English and German interface, following the macOS language preference
- 💻 Native macOS menu-bar app for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs
